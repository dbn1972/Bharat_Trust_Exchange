# BTX v0.1.0 — What's New (Citizen Summary)

**Date**: May 2026

---

## What is Bharat Trust Exchange?

Bharat Trust Exchange (BTX) is a platform that puts you in control of your personal data. You decide who can access your data, for what purpose, and for how long — and you can change your mind at any time.

---

## What's new in this release

### You are in control

- You can now **grant consent** to let trusted partners access specific data fields for a specific purpose. You choose what, to whom, and for how long.
- You can **revoke consent at any time** from your portal. When you revoke, all connected services are notified within seconds.
- You can **view your full history** — every time consent was granted, queried, or revoked is permanently recorded and cannot be altered.

### Your data stays safe

- All consent records are protected with strong encryption.
- Your identity is never stored in plain text — only a secure code is used internally.
- Every action is logged with a tamper-proof seal so no one can secretly change your history.

### Your rights are protected

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to:
- **See** all consents you have given
- **Correct** by revoking an old consent and granting a new one
- **Delete** — revocation is immediate; full data deletion follows the legal retention window
- **Export** your consent history as a file from the portal
- **Raise a grievance** — contact the BTX Grievance Officer through the portal; we respond within 21 days

### Your data stays in India

BTX stores all your data on servers located in India. No personal data is sent outside India in this release.

---

## Things we are still working on

- **Automatic expiry**: We are building a feature to automatically expire consents at their end date. Until then, consents must be revoked manually.
- **More languages**: We currently support English, Hindi, and Marathi. More regional languages are coming.
- **Guardian consent for minors**: Support for parental/guardian consent for users under 18 is being finalized.

---

## How to get help

- **Portal**: Log in at [btx.gov.in](https://btx.gov.in) (coming soon)
- **Grievance**: Use the "Raise a concern" button in the portal
- **Email**: grievance@btx.gov.in
- **Response time**: Within 21 days for grievances; urgent cases within 72 hours

---

*Bharat Trust Exchange is committed to protecting your privacy. This platform is built in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.*
