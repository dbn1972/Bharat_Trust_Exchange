## Wrong vs right

**Wrong** — flip producers to ML-DSA before all verifiers accept ML-DSA → broken signatures across federation
**Right** — phase 1: verifiers accept both; phase 2: producers opt-in by member class; phase 3: ed25519 producers retired

**Wrong** — delete old kid keys immediately after cutover
**Right** — retain old keys until retention boundary; verification of historical artefacts must remain possible
