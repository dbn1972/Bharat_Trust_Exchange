## Wrong vs right

**Wrong** — promote to rc with axis 7 = 7 ("close enough")
**Right** — rc requires every axis ≥ 8; either fix or stay at beta

**Wrong** — accept stale P-30 audit (30 days old) for beta
**Right** — `tools/audit-freshness` rejects upstream audits > 14d
