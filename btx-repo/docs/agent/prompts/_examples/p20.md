## Wrong vs right

**Wrong** — marketing language ("now blazingly fast, more secure than ever")
**Right** — factual claims with PR/ADR/CT links and numbers ("PDP p95 -18% under 1k rps — PR-1234, evidence/perf/...")

**Wrong** — public notes leak internal hostnames and IP ranges
**Right** — public variant sanitised; internal variant retains operator-relevant detail
