## Wrong vs right

**Wrong** — record sessions by default
**Right** — recording is opt-in; participant gets a copy of the consent form; recordings encrypted, retention ≤ 90 d

**Wrong** — use real Aadhaar numbers in test stub
**Right** — synthetic but realistic identifiers; clearly labelled "test environment" in UI

**Wrong** — only pay participants who completed all tasks
**Right** — full compensation regardless of completion; right to stop at any time

**Wrong** — Critical mis-share finding parked for "next sprint"
**Right** — Critical finding on a citizen surface blocks the next release until remediated or risk-accepted by `@privacy-lead`
